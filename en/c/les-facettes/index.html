Veneers

Thin pieces of ceramic, veneers cover the entire visible surface of the front teeth, and only that surface. They are used to enhance the smile by changing the shade and shape of the teeth.

In diagrams: fitting a veneer

They are used to

  • Improve the colour of the teeth, for example when whitening proves ineffective.
  • Hiding enamel stains: tetracyclines (an antibiotic) taken during childhood, excess fluoride (fluorosis), enamel defects…
  • Removing surface imperfections: cracks, small chips, porosity…
  • Closing gaps between the teeth, such as a gap between the front teeth.
  • Change the shape of teeth: incisors that are too short, lateral incisors that are small compared with the other teeth…

How it works

To place conventional veneers, the tooth needs to be reduced slightly: the surface enamel is gently trimmed, to the strict minimum and only on the visible side. This makes just enough room for the veneer without making the tooth bulkier, which would spoil the result. Once the teeth are prepared and impressions taken, temporary veneers are fitted. You wear them until the appointment at which the final veneers, made by the dental technician, are placed.

The result

The result is highly aesthetic and long-lasting. Ceramic is strong, does not stain and resists wear; the surrounding gum tolerates it perfectly. Your smile is “made to measure”, with teeth that regain the shade and translucency of healthy enamel.
