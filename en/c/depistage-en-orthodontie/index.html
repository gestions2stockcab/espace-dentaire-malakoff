Orthodontic screening

Early screening is very important. There is no standard age to start treatment.

In diagrams: the effect of thumb-sucking

The dental practitioner will decide, for each situation, the best time to start treatment, based on many factors. These relate to genetics (bone growth), the environment (finger sucking, mouth breathing, pushing the tongue between the teeth when swallowing…), the stage of development of the teeth (not always related to the child’s actual age), the severity of the deformity or deformities, and the combination of different deformities (very common in orthodontics, as a primary deformity leads to secondary ones). The child’s maturity and ability to cooperate must also be taken into account.

Sometimes treatment in two stages, separated by a monitoring period, will be recommended: first to prevent the problem from getting worse in a very young child, then to complete treatment once all the permanent teeth have come through.
