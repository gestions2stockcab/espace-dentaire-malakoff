Fixed braces systems

The traditional system

A few years ago, the only fixed braces system available in orthodontics consisted of:

  • steel brackets bonded to the visible surface of each tooth;
  • bands cemented to the last back tooth on each side;
  • an archwire running through all the bands and brackets: precise bending of this archwire over the appointments is what moves the teeth;
  • elastics that attach each bracket to the archwire.

New fixed braces systems

They were developed to improve the comfort, appearance and effectiveness of orthodontic treatment. Today, we can offer you, for example:

Clear brackets

Made from high-quality ceramic, they literally blend in with the colour of the tooth and are almost invisible. Their colour is not affected by staining foods and drinks (tea, coffee…). They are suitable for adults and teenagers, and even children.

Self-ligating braces

No more uncomfortable little elastics that lose their colour with food and trap plaque: a part built into the bracket clips the archwire in place while still letting it slide slightly, which helps the teeth move. The brackets are smaller than conventional ones. As well as looking better and being easier to keep clean, these appliances move teeth more efficiently, which shortens treatment and reduces the number of appointments. This system is suitable for adults and children alike.

Lingual techniques

The brackets are bonded to the inner surface of the teeth. Smaller than conventional brackets, they are custom-made using CAD (computer-aided design), as are the archwires. It is an effective system and the most discreet form of fixed braces, as it is completely invisible. It is reserved for adults and teenagers.

Today, wearing fixed braces is less restrictive and more discreet, and the goals are reached faster than with traditional systems. Talk to your orthodontist: they will advise you on the treatment best suited to your case and your wishes.

The brackets and archwire apply a gentle, continuous force: the bone remodels around the roots and the teeth move gradually.
