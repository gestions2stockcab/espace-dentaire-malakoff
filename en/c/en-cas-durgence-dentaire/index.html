Dental emergency in Malakoff

Pain, broken tooth, abscess, fall: call the centre on 01 85 62 62 00.

Call 15 immediately

In case of major facial swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or a violent blow.

Knocked-out adult tooth

Hold it by the crown without touching the root, rinse it briefly if it is dirty, keep it in milk or in your saliva, and see a dentist within the hour.

Advice sheets: Sport and mouthguards · After an extraction

Sources: IADT, Dental trauma guidelines

Further information

On-call service

The departmental French Dental Council (Ordre) runs an on-call service on Sundays and public holidays. You can get the details of the on-call dentist by calling the police station or gendarmerie in your town.

Dental trauma

In the event of dental trauma (after a fall, for example), please contact the practice by phone as soon as possible on +33 1 85 62 62 00, or the nearest hospital (dental department or oral and maxillofacial surgery department).

A question about this? The centre can help on +33 1 85 62 62 00, or you can book online at any time.
