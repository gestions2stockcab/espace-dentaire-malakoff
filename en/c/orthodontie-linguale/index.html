Lingual orthodontics

In lingual orthodontics, the brackets are fixed to the inner surface of the teeth, on the tongue side, so they remain completely hidden. No other orthodontic method is as aesthetic.

In diagrams: braces placed on the tongue side

Design

Placed on the tongue side, the brackets are designed and made specifically for each patient: each one must fit exactly the surface of the tooth it is attached to. The metal archwires are also custom-shaped, for greater precision and effectiveness.

Dedicated software is used. It records the shape, surface and position of the teeth as well as the characteristics of the jaw, then simulates the whole treatment on screen. This information is used to produce the brackets and the series of archwires by CAD, which are then sent to your orthodontist.

With the lingual method, most tooth misalignments can be corrected without the appliance being visible at any point during treatment.
