Dental crowns in Malakoff

Crowns and bridges at Espace dentaire Malakoff, 112 boulevard Gabriel Péri.

How it works

A crown covers and protects a badly damaged, broken or root-treated tooth. It is custom-made in ceramic or zirconia. If the tooth is very damaged, a post and core may first rebuild its base.

The steps

  1. Tooth preparation and impression; a temporary crown protects the tooth.
  2. Fitting the crown (final, custom-made).
