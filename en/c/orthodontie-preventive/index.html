Preventive orthodontics

At what age should you see an orthodontist?

Prevention is better than cure: the saying sums up preventive orthodontics, also called interceptive orthodontics.

In diagrams: the effect of thumb-sucking

The idea is simple: the earlier you act, the easier the correction. A child’s mouth can be examined from age 4, and their dentist or practitioner can be asked for an opinion. In any case, a first assessment before age 8 is strongly recommended.

Treatment

Several treatments can then be considered, depending on the diagnosis.
